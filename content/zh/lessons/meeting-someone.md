deeper:
  - question: Why not take these phrases apart?
    answer: Some use grammar that comes later. Learned whole, they work today.
  - question: What answers thanks or an apology?
    answer: 不客气 is a common answer to thanks, and 没关系 to an apology.
  - question: Do friends say 你好?
    answer: Less often. It suits strangers and first meetings. Friends tend to use a name or something casual.
  - question: When do I use 您?
    answer: It's the polite form of 你, for elders, teachers and anyone you want to show respect.

:::lesson
Greetings are set phrases, so you learn them whole. :w[你好] opens and :w[再见] closes. :w[谢谢] thanks someone. :ex[认识你很高兴。] is for a first meeting.
:::
