# sandbox

Temporary and experimental work: mockups, spikes, throwaway prototypes. Nothing in the app imports from here, and anything in it can be deleted once it has served its purpose. Decisions it leads to are recorded in `docs/`.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ui-mockups/index.html` | Static phone mockups of every main screen, for nailing the UI/UX before building. Open it in a browser. The proposal behind it is [docs/design/app-ui-flow.md](../docs/design/app-ui-flow.md). |
