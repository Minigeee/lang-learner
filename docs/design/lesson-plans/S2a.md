# S2a · Stage S2, first half: units 2.1–2.15

Lesson plans for S2 up to the midpoint checkpoint. The format and the docs that own each rule are in the [README](README.md). The units and ideas come from [doc 03 §1.8](../../research/03-mandarin-curriculum-and-materials.md), S2 table rows 2.1–2.15.

## Stage summary

### Path

- **2.1–2.5 · Aspect.** What happened (verb 了 with an amount, 没 without 了), what changed (sentence 了), ever (过), states and simultaneous actions (着, 一边), and results (完, 到, 见).
- **2.6–2.9 · Modals, requests, people, counting.** 要/想, 应该/得, 会 "will"; softening, 别 and 请/叫/让; 给, 跟, 对; measure words, 有的/一些, 每…都.
- **2.10–2.14 · Description, time and place.** 的 descriptions, time order (以前, 的时候, 已经, 刚), 就/才, place-first sentences, 来/去, getting around.
- **2.15 · Midpoint checkpoint.** Three checkpoints, each takeable alone.

**At the end, the learner can:**
- follow an HSK 2–3 learner story at normal learner speed with pinyin on peek, and tell what happened, what changed, what was ever done, and what state holds;
- tell apart by ear the pairs S2a teaches: 了/过, 着/在, 不/不…了, 要/想, 会 "will"/"can", 应该/得, 就/才, 来/去, 以前/以后, 已经/刚, 给/跟/对, 有的/一些;
- parse sentences where the order changes the meaning: who acts after 让, who receives after 给, what a 的 phrase describes, what sits or arrives at a place.

**Defaults that apply to every lesson below** (stated once here, not per lesson):
- **Arc:** opener → lesson (I do) → examples (we do) → practice (you do) → end card. No synthesis or replay step ([brainstorm §4.1](../activity-brainstorm.md)).
- **Skill card:** every lesson's skill card reviews in that lesson's practice format, on unseen stimuli (§4.4).
- **Audio:** sentence items come from AISHELL-3 or AISHELL-1. ElevenLabs v4 fills thin pools and voices every LLM-generated sentence. Each lesson's Media line names only the exceptions.
- **Voices:** AISHELL-3 alone has 218 speakers, so every contrast gets ≥4 voices, and the you do uses voices not heard in that lesson.
- **Support stage:** hanzi always on (S2 is "read alongside audio"). Pinyin fades across each unit: on → peek → off, and replays go from unlimited to limited.
- **Rungs:** grammar lessons climb phrase → sentence (→ exchange where a lesson uses Q&A). No S2a lesson reaches the clip-line rung: that needs a transcript we may use, and none of the candidate clips has one (see Video notes).
- **Clip lessons** (2.3c, 2.5d, 2.10c) follow S1's rule: a YouTube clip, where there is one, is a `supplemental` gist opener, and the pass-2 read-along and practice run on an LLM-written story voiced by ElevenLabs v4 (rung: story line).

#### 2.1a · 了 plus an amount: the action is done   (les:le-done-amount)
- Shape: Pattern · Skill: verb + 了 + amount reports a finished action (zh:g:le-verb-amount) · ~6 min
- Teacher's summary: S1 (1.17b) met verb + 了 in stories. The S2 point is the amount: 买了三本书 reports one finished event and sounds complete. Practice sets it against a plan (要) and a habit (每天), the two readings learners confuse it with.
- Opener (Discover): five or six done-with-an-amount sentences. *What do they share?* (three options).
- Lesson (I do): verb + 了 + an amount reports one finished event. X12 timeline figure: ✔ before now, with the amount as a closed box. No ✗/✓: a bare 我买了书 isn't wrong, only open-ended (Go deeper).
- Examples (we do): AISHELL sentences across shopping, eating and reading contexts; 了 lit; meaning one tap away. Rung: phrase → sentence.
- Practice (you do): C0 Pick, timeline face (✔ done · ▶ plan · ⟳ habit). The learner tells 买了三本 from 要买三本 and 每天买三本: the verb and amount stay the same, and only the marker (了, 要, 每天) differs. Rungs: phrase → sentence. It fits because the cue is a meaning on the timeline the learner already knows.
  Skill card: same format.
- Variety: Discover · C0 · phrase → sentence · 3 contexts · interleaves 要 "going to" (1.11) · pinyin on.
- Media: I Heart Mandarin (supplemental, recap after practice); AISHELL.
- Notes: Go deeper holds "了 isn't past tense" and "why a bare 我买了书 sounds unfinished" (it expects more: an amount, a sentence-final 了 or a next clause).

#### 2.1b · 没 drops the 了   (les:mei-drops-le)
- Shape: Pattern · Skill: 没 + verb negates a finished action, and 了吗? is answered 没有 (zh:g:mei-no-le) · ~6 min
- Teacher's summary: the learner already knows 没 means "didn't" (1.15). The new point is that in a "didn't" sentence the 了 drops: 我买了 becomes 我没买. Then the exchange 你吃了吗? → 没有 / 吃了.
- Opener (Showdown): 他买了 / 他没买, same voice. *Which one happened?*
- Lesson (I do): 没 already says "didn't happen", so 了 drops. ✗/✓: 我没买了 / 我没买. Figure: X12, ✔ vs ✖.
- Examples (we do): question–answer pairs (了吗? → 没有 / verb + 了) from 4+ voices; then mixed with 不 sentences.
- Practice (you do): C3 Catch the mismatch. Three or four lines with English; one English line flips *did* ↔ *didn't*. The Chinese is always right. Rungs: sentence → short exchange. It fits because the cue is exactly the 了/没 meaning.
  Skill card: same format.
- Variety: Showdown · C3 · sentence → exchange · Q&A context · interleaves 不/没 (1.15) · pinyin peek.
- Media: Grace (supplemental); AISHELL.
- Notes: 没 and 了 do meet in other patterns: 没钱了 (2.2's change-了) and "not for a while" sentences (好久没见了). Keep both out of the items; Go deeper names them so the rule isn't read as "never together".

#### 2.2a · 了 after a time or age says it's reached   (les:le-time-age-reached)
- Shape: Pattern · Skill: time, age or stage + 了 = it has been reached now (zh:g:le-change-time-age) · ~6 min
- Teacher's summary: second pass on 1.17a's final 了, which met verbs and adjectives (下雨了, 我饿了). New here: with a time, an age or a stage (十点了, 我二十岁了, 大学生了) there's no verb at all, so a learner listening for an action misses the 了. Frame it as "it's got to… now". 天黑了 bridges from S1.
- Opener (Mystery): *Why 了 in 我二十岁了? Nobody did anything.*
- Lesson (I do): a time or age + 了 says it has been reached. X12: a line that crosses a mark at "now". ✗/✓ gloss: 我二十岁了 as "I was twenty" / "I'm twenty now".
- Examples (we do): clock times, ages, seasons and school stages, many voices, 了 lit.
- Practice (you do): C2 Odd one out. Four voices; three end in change-了 and one states the same kind of fact plainly (现在十点 among three 了 lines). Rungs: phrase → sentence. It fits because the whole skill is catching a neutral-tone 了 at the end.
  Skill card: same format.
- Variety: Mystery · C2 · phrase → sentence · time, age, school · interleaves final 了 (1.17a), verb-了 (2.1a) · pinyin on.
- Media: Yoyo (supplemental, seen in 1.17a); AISHELL.
- Notes: C2's four clips are different sentences. The odd one is decided by the form only, never by voice. Reframed from the draft (see decomposition); the old slug duplicated 1.17a's.

#### 2.2b · 不…了 means not any more   (les:bu-le-not-anymore)
- Shape: Pattern · Skill: 不 + verb + 了 = stopped or changed plan (zh:g:bu-le) · ~6 min
- Teacher's summary: a minimal pair the learner meets constantly in dramas: 我不去 vs 我不去了. The 了 turns a refusal into a change of mind.
- Opener (Showdown): 我不去 / 我不去了, same voice, from AISHELL. *Which one changed their mind?*
- Lesson (I do): 不…了 is 2.2a's "new situation", in the negative. ✗/✓ glosses: "I don't go" / "I'm not going any more".
- Examples (we do): smoking, eating, going and waiting sentences, 4+ voices; then mixed with plain 不.
- Practice (you do): C3 Catch the mismatch: English flips *won't* ↔ *not any more*. Rungs: sentence → short exchange.
  Skill card: same format.
- Variety: Showdown · C3 · sentence → exchange · habits and plans · interleaves 不 (1.3), 2.2a · pinyin peek.
- Media: AISHELL (2.2-b: 124 sentences).
- Notes: none.

#### 2.2c · 快…了 says it's about to happen   (les:kuai-le-about-to)
- Shape: Pattern · Skill: 快/要/就要 + verb + 了 = about to happen (zh:g:kuai-yao-le) · ~6 min
- Teacher's summary: the trap is reading 快 as "fast". The frame 快…了 points just ahead of now. 就要 often comes with a time word; 快 doesn't.
- Opener (Mystery): *快下雨了: is the rain fast?*
- Lesson (I do): X12, ▶ just ahead of now. ✗/✓: "it rains fast" / "it's about to rain". Go deeper: 就要 can take a time word, 快 can't.
- Examples (we do): trains, rain, holidays and class ending, 4+ voices.
- Practice (you do): C0 Pick, timeline face (▶ about to · ✔ already changed). It contrasts 快…了 with 2.2a's change-了. Rungs: phrase → sentence.
  Skill card: same format.
- Variety: Mystery · C0 · phrase → sentence · daily life · interleaves 2.2a, 要 "going to" (1.11) · pinyin peek.
- Media: CZH (supplemental); AISHELL.
